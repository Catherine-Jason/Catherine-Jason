Overview

This lab shows how I prepared and deployed devices during the district rollout, including refreshing old devices and handling the ones that were retired.

Real world relevance: A device that is imaged and updated the same way every time is more secure and gets to the user faster.

Approach

  1. I prepared devices with the district image and standard settings.
  2. I ran imaging and checked that updates and security patches were installed.
  3. I kept work moving by unboxing and prepping the next batch while the current one reimaged.
  4. I checked enrollment, software, and sign in before a device went out.
  5. I handled end of life devices by wiping them, checking them, and tagging them as retired.

Key Skills Demonstrated

  • Deploying devices in large numbers
  • Imaging devices the same way every time
  • Checking updates and enrollment before handoff
  • Handling devices at the end of their life
