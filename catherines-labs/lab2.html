Lab 2 — Inventory & Asset Management

Case study on district device lifecycle tracking, assignment validation, and compliance-focused asset management.

Overview

This lab showcases enterprise asset workflows supporting large fleet operations, including 6000+ student devices and 45+ staff systems.

Real-world relevance: Accurate inventory data supports security, compliance, and rapid support response across distributed campuses.

Approach

  1. Located assets by serial number, TS tag, or user assignment records.
  2. Validated ownership and current lifecycle state in district systems.
  3. Applied compliance and update workflows before redeployment.
  4. Documented changes and synchronized status across management platforms.
  5. Prepared devices for refresh, reassignment, or end-of-life processing.

Key Skills Demonstrated

  • Enterprise inventory operations and record accuracy control
  • Lifecycle management and deployment readiness checks
  • Compliance tracking and endpoint policy enforcement
  • Cross-team coordination for district-wide device rollout

Tools & Technologies

  • CCPS Inventory System
  • Microsoft Intune policies
  • Dell Command Update
  • Imaging and staging workflows

Results & Outcomes

  • Maintained accurate asset visibility for high-volume district environments.
  • Supported compliance posture and readiness through policy-driven updates.
  • Improved deployment throughput by standardizing imaging and staging steps.
