Lab 5 - Device Deployment & Imaging

Real-world district-scale device imaging, unboxing, configuration, and deployment for CCPS (Chesterfield County Public Schools) covering 6,000+ student and staff devices across 43 school sites.

Overview

This lab documents the full device rollout I was part of at CCPS. Working alongside three other IT employees, we reimaged and deployed over 6,000 student laptops across 43 school sites in the district - all completed within four weeks. This was a full-cycle operation that included unboxing, imaging, applying Windows security updates, installing required student applications, and prepping devices for distribution.

In addition to student devices, we also handled 43 teacher and administration devices that went through the same process - unboxing, reimaging, applying updates, and getting them fully configured and ready for use.

Real-world relevance: Large-scale endpoint deployment requires consistency, coordination, and attention to detail. Every device had to meet the same compliance standard before it left our hands.

Approach

  1. Unboxed and physically inspected incoming student and staff devices before starting any imaging process.
  2. Applied the district baseline image to each device, ensuring consistent configuration across all 43 sites.
  3. Ran Windows security updates and verified each device was fully patched before deployment.
  4. Installed required student applications through Company Portal and district-approved software packages.
  5. Verified device enrollment in district management systems and confirmed sign-in and app availability.
  6. Staged completed devices for site-specific rollout distribution across the district.

Key Skills Demonstrated

  • High-volume endpoint imaging and deployment at district scale
  • Windows security update management and compliance verification
  • Application deployment and student software readiness checks
  • Device unboxing, physical inspection, and lifecycle intake procedures
  • Team coordination across a fast-paced rollout environment
  • Site-based staging and organized distribution workflows for 43 locations

Tools & Technologies

  • Windows 11 imaging and installation media
  • Microsoft Intune policy enforcement
  • Dell Command Update
  • Company Portal and Software Center
  • District asset lifecycle and inventory systems

Results & Outcomes

  • Successfully reimaged and deployed 6,000+ student laptops across 43 CCPS school sites within four weeks.
  • Processed 43 teacher and administration devices through the same full imaging and configuration workflow.
  • Every device shipped out fully patched, enrolled, and application-ready before reaching schools.
  • Maintained consistent deployment standards across the entire district fleet through coordinated team effort.
