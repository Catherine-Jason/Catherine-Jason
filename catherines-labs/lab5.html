Lab 5 — Device Deployment & Imaging

Case study of district-scale imaging, configuration, and deployment operations for student and staff endpoints.

Overview

This lab demonstrates large-scale endpoint preparation and deployment as part of district rollout operations, including refresh and end-of-life handling.

Real-world relevance: Consistent imaging and deployment workflows protect device security posture while enabling rapid technology distribution.

Approach

  1. Unboxed and prepared student, staff, and lab systems for district rollout and refresh operations.
  2. Applied district imaging standards, using UEFI Secure Boot for student devices and Ethernet-based imaging through Onboard NIC (IPv4) for staff systems and lab desktops.
  3. Executed large-scale reimaging cycles with policy alignment, update verification, and deployment staging by site.
  4. Validated enrollment, software readiness, compliance status, and account sign-in before handoff.
  5. Managed end-of-life workflows including wipe, audit, and retirement tagging.

Key Skills Demonstrated

  • Large-scale endpoint deployment planning and execution
  • Standardized imaging and configuration control
  • Quality checks for compliance, updates, and enrollment readiness
  • Hardware lifecycle and retirement management

Tools & Technologies

  • Imaging workflow tools and deployment checklists
  • Microsoft Intune policy enforcement
  • Dell Command Update
  • Company Portal and Software Center readiness checks
  • District asset lifecycle systems

Results & Outcomes

  • Supported district rollout and refresh efforts with repeatable imaging and deployment standards at scale.
  • Improved handoff readiness by validating endpoint compliance, updates, and enrollment pre-delivery.
  • Demonstrated structured end-of-life management for secure device retirement.
