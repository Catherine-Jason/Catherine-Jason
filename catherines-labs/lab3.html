Lab 3: Account and Access Administration

IT Support Casework: Identity and Access Management

Identity and access management case study covering account controls, MFA workflows, and permission governance.

Home Catherine's Labs

Overview

This lab shows how I handle account problems while keeping people working and following the rules. It reflects the account work in my current role.

Real world relevance: Accounts are one of the first things attackers go after, so every change to one has to be careful.

Approach

  1. I confirmed who the person was and that the request was real before changing anything.
  2. I reset passwords and MFA through the approved steps.
  3. I updated user and group settings when the person needed different access.
  4. I tested the login to make sure access worked again.
  5. I wrote down every change that touched security.

Key Skills Demonstrated

  • Safe account recovery after checking identity
  • Managing users and groups in Active Directory
  • Helping with MFA and password resets
  • Matching a person's access to their role
Identity Verification MFA Administration Active Directory Role Based Access Control

Tools & Technologies

  • Active Directory
  • The MFA security info portal
  • The district User Management System
  • Group and permission settings

Results & Outcomes

  • I fixed account lockouts and MFA problems without loosening security.
  • I kept notes on identity and access changes so they can be checked later.
