Lab 1: Helpdesk Ticketing Workflow

IT Support Casework: Ticket Triage and Resolution

Case study focused on real district helpdesk triage and troubleshooting workflows that support day to day technology operations.

Home Catherine's Labs

Overview

This lab shows how I work a support ticket from the first message to the final note. It is based on the help desk work I do for staff and students at Carroll County Public Schools.

Real world relevance: When tickets are sorted well, classrooms and staff spend less time waiting on a broken account or device, and access stays secure.

Approach

  1. I read the ticket to see who it affects and how urgent it is.
  2. I confirm who the person is before I touch anything on their account.
  3. I use remote tools to see the problem myself and find the cause.
  4. I fix the MFA, password, or lockout issue and check that the person can get in.
  5. I write down what I did and the final status so the next person can pick it up.

Key Skills Demonstrated

  • Sorting tickets by urgency
  • Checking a person's identity before account changes
  • Remote troubleshooting and walking users through a fix
  • Clear notes when a ticket is closed

Tools & Technologies

  • Quick Assist
  • Remote Desktop
  • Active Directory
  • Company Portal
  • The district ticketing system

Results & Outcomes

  • I reset MFA and unlocked accounts after confirming who was asking.
  • I helped people recover passwords without weakening account security.
  • The goal every time was to get the person back to work or class quickly.
