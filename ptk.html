About This Honor

Phi Theta Kappa is an international honor society that recognizes academic achievement among students at associate-degree-granting institutions. Membership reflects sustained classroom performance, commitment to scholarship, and active academic engagement.

Being recognized through the Alpha Psi Psi Chapter highlights my dedication to continued learning while building technical skills in cybersecurity, networking, and IT support. It represents both academic discipline and a commitment to professional growth.
