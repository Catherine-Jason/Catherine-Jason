Lab 1 — Helpdesk Ticketing Workflow

This lab demonstrates how I handle real helpdesk tickets including triage, troubleshooting, user verification, and resolution using district tools.

Tools Used

  • Helpdesk Online
  • Microsoft Apps Portal

Process

  1. Reviewed the ticket details and user information.
  2. Verified the user’s identity and access level.
  3. Troubleshot login or access issues.
  4. Documented the resolution and closed the ticket.
