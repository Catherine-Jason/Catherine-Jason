SOC Alert Triage Demo

An interactive browser-based simulation that puts you in the role of a Tier 1 SOC analyst. You review a suspicious login alert, investigate the context, and decide how to respond, just like you would in a real Security Operations Center.

What I Built

  • A complete decision-tree alert triage workflow in pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • Simulated alert dashboard with realistic login event data
  • Branching response paths that show consequences of each analyst decision
  • Educational feedback at each step to reinforce SOC procedures
