About CompTIA Security+

I chose CompTIA Security+ because it is a recognized entry level cybersecurity certification. It is vendor neutral and validates core skills needed for security operations and IT security roles.

The SY0-701 exam covers risk management, threat analysis, incident response, and enterprise security architecture. It is approved by the U.S. Department of Defense and aligns with ISO 17024 standards.

This certification confirms my foundation in security concepts and my ability to assess and respond to security incidents in practical environments.
