About CompTIA Security+

CompTIA Security+ is one of the most recognized entry-level cybersecurity certifications in the industry. It is a vendor-neutral credential that validates the skills and knowledge required to perform core security functions and pursue a career in IT security.

The SY0-701 exam covers the latest trends and techniques in risk management, threat analysis, incident response, and enterprise network architecture. It is approved by the U.S. Department of Defense and meets ISO 17024 standards.

Earning this certification demonstrates a solid foundation in security concepts and the practical ability to assess and respond to security incidents in a real-world environment.

Key Domains Covered

  • General Security Concepts
  • Threats, Vulnerabilities & Mitigations
  • Security Architecture
  • Security Operations
  • Security Program Management & Oversight
  • Cryptography & PKI
  • Identity & Access Management
  • Incident Response Lifecycle
