Lab 2: Inventory and Asset Management

IT Support Casework: Device Lifecycle and Compliance Tracking

Case study on district device lifecycle tracking, assignment validation, and compliance focused asset management.

Overview

This lab shows how I kept track of district devices during the summer rollout. I helped deploy about 6,000 student devices and about 45 administration and teacher devices.

Real world relevance: If the inventory is accurate, it is easy to find a device, know who has it, and keep it secure and up to date.

Approach

  1. I found each device by serial number, TS tag, or the person it was assigned to.
  2. I checked who owned it and what stage of its life it was in.
  3. I ran updates before a device was handed out again.
  4. I recorded the changes so the inventory matched the device.
  5. I got devices ready to be refreshed, reassigned, or retired.

Key Skills Demonstrated

  • Keeping inventory records accurate
  • Tracking a device from deployment to retirement
  • Checking that devices are updated and follow policy
  • Working with other team members across many sites
Asset Lifecycle Management Compliance Tracking Endpoint Policy Enforcement Fleet Deployment

Tools & Technologies

  • The district inventory system
  • Microsoft Intune
  • Dell Command Update
  • Imaging and staging steps

Results & Outcomes

  • At the end of each cycle I scanned devices into inventory by site, so the records stayed current.
  • Devices got their updates before they went out.
  • Our team reached the goal of about 6,000 new devices in under four weeks.
